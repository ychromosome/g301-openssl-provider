# Working rules for inner threads

This subtree is provisionally all rights reserved under
`LicenseRef-G301-Inner-Reserved` pending legal review. Do not add Apache-2.0
headers or copy files into the Apache-licensed outer subtree.

Preserve five channels, two directions, independent derivation, canonical
framing, nonce uniqueness, strict limits, fail-closed behavior, and zeroizing
secret storage. Use maintained OpenSSL primitives; do not implement crypto.

No Q2, QUIC, READY, record type `0x83`, lore text, raw-exporter application API,
or process-global-libctx TLS adapter may be added.
